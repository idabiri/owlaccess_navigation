## Important notes

- The indoor interior of BLD001 is **synthetic** (invented) and must keep its "synthetic" labels. It does not describe the real building.
- The entrance and ramp points were hand-digitized by the project team using Kennesaw State University's published accessibility map as a visual reference. They are **unverified**.
- **Not covered by this licence and not in this repository:** Kennesaw State University's maps (`data/reference/ksu_maps/`, git-ignored). They remain KSU's property.
